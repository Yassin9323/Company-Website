🌟ALX Pulse

"From self-started to an official ALX collaboration — a vision born from purpose, built to empower."

ALX Pulse is a dynamic community management platform crafted by the Rockets Team to empower and streamline the ALX ecosystem. What began as a self-initiated, zero-funding project quickly evolved into a scalable solution designed to connect ALX teams, alumni, and initiatives under one unified hub. Driven by our deep engagement with the ALX community, we identified a clear need for better coordination, centralized communication, and opportunity visibility. Instead of waiting, we built — launching a platform to meet real community needs. After successfully pitching our MVP and long-term vision, we secured an official partnership and budget support from ALX, enabling us to scale the platform and expand its capabilities.
